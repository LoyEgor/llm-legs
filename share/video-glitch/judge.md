# Judge one AI-generated video clip for generation defects, structure inventory (__HANDLE__)

This is a read-only visual inspection: open the images, write nothing, run no commands, no tests.

Below are __N__ frames of ONE clip generated by a video model, sampled at __FPS__ frames per second in time order.
Each frame is labelled `t=<seconds>` in its top-left corner. Look at every frame at full size, one by one.

__FRAMES__

Video models change things smoothly, so a defect often looks plausible between two neighbouring frames and only
shows when a later frame is compared with an earlier one. Work in two steps.

Step 1, inventory. From the first frames, note each rigid structure that matters in the shot (doors, windows,
frames, furniture, devices, vehicles, signs, tools, objects on a table) and how it is built, with its countable
parts: leaves, panels, frame bars, handles, hinges, legs, screens, how it opens. Then compare every later frame
with that inventory. Defects:
- a part that disappears, appears, duplicates or changes type without a visible cause (nobody carries, opens or
  covers it), even when the change is smooth;
- a part that moves in a way its build does not allow: a hinged door that slides, a leaf that passes through its
  frame or a fixed panel, a frame that stretches or slides away;
- an object that moves by itself while nobody touches it (a door that opens before the person reaches it).

Step 2, people and text. Also report: a limb bent at an impossible angle or joint; a hand rotated against its
forearm; a limb missing, doubled, or fused into an object; a melted or distorted face; a person turning
translucent or passing through glass, a wall or a frame; letters or a logo that are malformed or change shape.

NOT defects: motion blur, depth of field, reflections and see-through glass, lens flare, grain, compression
noise, planned camera or object motion, ordinary perspective and parallax, a part hidden behind something or
out of frame, stylised but consistent design.

Rules: report a defect only if you can point to it in specific frames and describe it concretely. When in
doubt, leave it out. Missing a small defect is acceptable; reporting something that is not a defect is a
serious error.

For each defect also say what the person ordering the clip should change in the start image or the prompt so
the next generation avoids it (a simpler object, a different motion, the door already open, ...).

Return exactly this and nothing else:
```
INVENTORY: <the main structures and their parts, one line, at most 40 words>
VERDICT: ok
```
or
```
INVENTORY: <the main structures and their parts, one line, at most 40 words>
VERDICT: defects
t=<first s>-<last s> | <object> | <what is wrong, at most 15 words> | noticeable: yes|no | confidence: high|medium | fix: <what to change, at most 15 words>
```
one `t=` line per defect, at most 6 lines.
