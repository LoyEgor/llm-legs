# Judge one AI-generated video clip for generation defects (__HANDLE__)

This is a read-only visual inspection: open the images, write nothing, run no commands, no tests.

Below are __N__ frames of ONE clip generated by a video model, sampled at 2 frames per second in time order.
Each frame is labelled `t=<seconds>` in its top-left corner. Look at every frame at full size, one by one.

__FRAMES__

Report only defects typical of generative video that a viewer would notice at normal playback speed:
1. Bodies: a limb bent at an impossible angle or joint; a hand rotated against its forearm or coming out of
   the sleeve the wrong way round; a limb missing, doubled, or fused into an object or another limb; a clearly
   wrong number of fingers; a melted or distorted face; a person turning translucent or merging into glass,
   wall or furniture.
2. Rigid objects: something that should be rigid (door, frame, furniture, vehicle, sign, tool) that is bent,
   melted, doubled into overlapping copies of itself, or fused into another object; a structure whose type
   changes between frames (for example a double swing door that becomes a revolving door or a stack of
   overlapping door leaves).
3. Text and logos: letters that are malformed or that change shape between frames.
4. Between frames: a person or object that appears or vanishes without a cause, changes identity (face,
   clothes, colour, shape), or a door or object that moves by itself while nobody touches it.

NOT defects: motion blur, depth of field, reflections and see-through glass, lens flare, grain, compression
noise, planned camera or object motion, ordinary perspective, a person partly out of frame or hidden behind
something, stylised but consistent design.

Rules: report a defect only if you can point to it in specific frames and describe it concretely. When in
doubt, leave it out. Missing a small defect is acceptable; reporting something that is not a defect is a
serious error.

Return exactly this and nothing else after the outcome line:
```
VERDICT: ok
```
or
```
VERDICT: defects
t=<first s>-<last s> | <object> | <what is wrong, at most 15 words> | noticeable: yes|no | confidence: high|medium
```
one `t=` line per defect, at most 6 lines.
